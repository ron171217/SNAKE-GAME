How to play

  • Press the arrow keys or WASD to move the snake.
  • Use the on-screen buttons or swipe on touch devices.
  • Collect fruit to grow longer and increase your score.
  • Each fruit speeds the game up slightly—stay sharp!
  • Colliding with the walls or your tail ends the game.
